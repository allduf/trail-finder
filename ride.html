How to get your Strava archive
  1. On a computer or in your phone's browser, sign in at strava.com (the Strava app can't do this step).
  2. Tap your photo at the top right, then Settings.
  3. Open My Account on the left.
  4. Under Download or Delete Your Account, press Get Started.
  5. In step 2, Download Request, press Request Your Archive. Don't touch the delete part.
  6. Strava emails you a download link, usually within a few hours. Download the .zip file.
  7. Come back here and press Import whole Strava archive, then pick that .zip. No need to unzip it.

    Rides are saved on this device, and in your account when you're signed in. Use Download GPX on a ride to keep a copy or upload it to Strava or Trailforks.

    Without connecting Strava: open the activity, ⋯ menu, Export GPX, then Import rides here. For all of them at once, use Import whole Strava archive (see how to get it above). Garmin and Wahoo .fit files work too. Imported rides show on the main map with My rides, which marks the trails you've ridden.
