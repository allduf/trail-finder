Trail Finder is one map app for outdoor sports in Canada: mountain biking, skiing, hiking, fishing, hunting and adventure moto. It's still being tested, and we're looking for people around Sea to Sky and beyond to try it and tell us what's missing.

Use the email of your Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android): the test invite is sent there.

Invites are sent by hand, in small groups, so it can take a few days.

  • iPhone: you'll get an email from TestFlight. Install the free TestFlight app, then tap the link in the email.
  • Android: you'll get an email with a Google Play link to join the test. Stay in the test for at least 14 days if you can: Google needs that before the app can be released.

Once you're in, More → Send feedback in the app goes straight to us.

The app is a test version: things may change or break, and trail, weather and avalanche information comes from public sources and may be out of date or wrong. Always check local conditions and rules yourself.
