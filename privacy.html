Privacy

Last updated October 27, 2026

Trail Finder is a map app for mountain biking, skiing, hunting, fishing and adventure riding. You can use all of it without an account. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and nothing is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Your location

Your location is used on your phone to show where you are, find trails near you, follow a ride or tour and give directions. It isn't sent to us unless you save something that holds it: a recorded ride, a spot, a trail report or a photo search (see below).

Without an account

  • Saved on your phone only: your settings, To ride and Ridden lists, folders, planned routes, recorded rides, catches, hunts and offline maps. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Visit count: the website sends a random device code once a day so we know roughly how many people use it. Nothing else is in it.
  • How the app is used (iPhone app only): a random device code, the phone model and iOS version, and which screens and buttons are opened, plus error messages, so we can find bugs and make the app easier to use. No name, email, location or photo.
  • Notifications: if you turn on alerts, your phone's notification address and the trails, tours or spots you watch are saved so the alerts can be sent. Turning alerts off deletes them.

With an account (optional)

Signing in keeps your settings, saved routes, rides and their photos, spots and folders on every device. We keep your email address and those things. Only you can read them.

Things you choose to share

  • Trail reports, grades, rider photos and trails you add are shown to other riders, without your email.
  • Help improve Trail Finder (off unless you turn it on): a trimmed copy of each ride's line (no start or end, only the month) is shared without any account link, to find unmapped trails and which way trails are ridden. You can take every ride back in My rides.
  • Which trail is this? (photo search): the photo or video frame, its GPS spot if it has one, and what you type are sent to Google (Gemini and Cloud Vision) to find the trail. When you confirm a match, a small copy of the picture is kept privately to make the next searches better. It is never shown to anyone.

Other services the app talks to

Like any map app, the app asks map, terrain and weather services for the area on your screen: OpenStreetMap, OpenFreeMap, Esri, Open-Meteo, Avalanche Canada, government open data and similar. They see the area and your internet address, never your account. Accounts and saved things are stored with Supabase. Strava files you import are read on your phone.

Deleting your data

Signed in, open More → Account → Settings → Delete my account. It deletes your account and everything saved to it, right away. To delete what's only on your phone, delete the app. For anything else, write to trailfinder.help@outlook.com.

Children

Trail Finder isn't made for children under 13 and we don't knowingly keep their data.

Contact

Questions about your data: trailfinder.help@outlook.com.
