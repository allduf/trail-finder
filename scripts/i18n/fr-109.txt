# Hunting trips (hunting-trip.js), 2026-11-17
Hunting: plan a hunting trip with Trips. Pick the camp, see each day's wind, legal light and moon, the seasons the app has read for your zone on your dates, the group's licences and tags, and a hunt log of what everyone saw and took. Shared only by private link, never public. ||| Chasse : planifiez un voyage de chasse avec Voyages. Partagé seulement par lien privé, jamais public.
